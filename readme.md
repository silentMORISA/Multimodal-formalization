# Datasets
1. UniGeo
- Source paper: [UniGeo: Unifying Geometry Logical Reasoning via Reformulating Mathematical Expression](https://arxiv.org/abs/2212.02746)
- Website: https://github.com/chen-judge/UniGeo
- Details: Contains 4,998 calculation problems and 9,543 proving problems.

2. Geometry3K
- Source paper: [Interpretable Geometry Problem Solving
with Formal Language and Symbolic Reasoning](<./Geometry problem solving/Lu 等 - 2021 - Inter-GPS Interpretable Geometry Problem Solving with Formal Language and Symbolic Reasoning.pdf>)
- Website: https://lupantech.github.io/inter-gps/
- Details: Consisting of 3,002 geometry choice problems with dense annotation in formal language.

3. PGPS9K
- Source paper: [A Multi-Modal Neural Geometric Solver with Textual Clauses Parsed from Diagram](<./Geometry problem solving/Zhang 等 - 2023 - A Multi-Modal Neural Geometric Solver with Textual Clauses Parsed from Diagram.pdf>)
- Website: https://huggingface.co/datasets/PeijieWang/PGPS9K
- Details: Composed of 9,022 geometry problems paired with non-duplicate 4,000 geometry diagrams, where 2,891 problems paired with 1,738 diagrams are selected from Geometry3K dataset, the rest of problems are collected from five popular textbooks across grades 6-12 on mathematics curriculum websites.

# Benchmark
1. PhyX-AF
- Source paper: [MMFormalizer: Multimodal Autoformalization in the Wild](<./Multimodal LEAN formalization/Xiong 等 - 2026 - MMFormalizer Multimodal Autoformalization in the Wild.pdf>)
- Website: https://huggingface.co/datasets/menik1126/PhyX-AF
- Details: Comprising 115 curated samples from MathVerse, PhyX, Synthetic Geometry, and Analytic Geometry.
